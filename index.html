About me

A bit of background.

I earned my bachelor's degree in Computer Science from Bells University of Technology in Nigeria, my master's degree in Mobile and Satellite Communication from the University of South Wales, United Kingdom, and a Ph.D. from the University of Missouri-Kansas City, United States.

From 2022 to 2024, I helped students at UMKC build the computer science foundation they needed before starting their master’s studies. I taught Discrete Structures, Operating Systems, and Data Structures and Algorithms. Earlier in my career, I worked on enterprise systems and public-infrastructure projects.
