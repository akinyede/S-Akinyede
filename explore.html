Case study / 02

AI that holds up
outside the lab.

CrossSense is my doctoral research on quality-aware AI for cross-domain sensing, connecting Bluetooth proximity sensing and agricultural edge vision.

Doctoral researchQuality-aware AICross-domain sensing
Explore signal qualitySynthetic illustration

Move the slider to explore the input before a prediction is made.

Noise is in arbitrary drawing units. This is not CrossSense inference, a calibrated reliability boundary, or a safety decision.

One framework. Domain-specific implementation.

CrossSense brings together quality estimation, context inference, signal stabilization, task inference, uncertainty calibration, and risk-limiting decision policy. The shared framework does not imply that models, thresholds, or calibration transfer unchanged.

Explore the six elements
  1. Quality estimation. Assess the input.
  2. Context inference. Understand the setting.
  3. Signal stabilization. Address fluctuations where appropriate.
  4. Task inference. Make the domain-specific prediction.
  5. Uncertainty calibration. Interpret confidence using evidence.
  6. Risk-limiting policy. Connect uncertainty to the response.
Research context and scope

The dissertation studies Bluetooth proximity sensing and agricultural edge vision. This page introduces the research program; the synthetic demonstration is not an experimental result.

Related work includes AlertBLE, AIBLE, and cross-architecture agricultural IoT vision research.

Case study / 03

Explore the trade-offs.
Make the choices explicit.

I independently conceived and delivered the Universal Metallurgical Process Simulator, connecting engineering calculations, multi-objective optimization, and financial analysis.

Industrial optimizationConcept & deliveryDecision support
Choose a perspectiveConceptual · not simulator output

Recovery is one part of the decision.

The simulator explores recovery alongside cost and environmental impact, rather than presenting one objective in isolation.

From process models to decision support.

The system combines physics-based mass and energy balances, genetic algorithms, NSGA-II Pareto exploration, and feasibility validation. Its financial layer includes CAPEX, OPEX, NPV, and IRR analysis.

Architecture and methods

I also built sensitivity and Monte Carlo uncertainty analysis, LLM explanations, and Flask/REST progress streaming. The work joins calculations, exploration, and a usable software interface.

What is—and is not—claimed

The supplied résumé supports the simulator’s methods and delivery. It does not give an independently verified recovery improvement, cost saving, or industrial deployment outcome. The diagram is conceptual, not a plot of measured process performance.
